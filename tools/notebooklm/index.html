NotebookLM: what it does, and when to try it.

NotebookLM is Google’s research and note-taking tool oriented around information you provide as sources.

Things you could use NotebookLM for

  • Explore notes and supplied documents
  • Organize research around source material
  • Draft questions to investigate further

For a first experiment, choose a task you can evaluate yourself: a draft, design concept, research question or technical example. Compare the outcome with your own quality standards before using it in public or professional work.

Potential advantages

  • Source-centered research workflow
  • Useful for document exploration

What to watch for

  • Outputs still need to be checked against your sources
