AI tools for students: research, revision and learning

A useful student workflow uses AI to create practice questions, summarize your own notes and explore explanations. It should not replace reading the source material, citing original work or following school and university rules.

Independent editorial guidance, updated 8 November 2026. Examples are illustrative exercises, not published hands-on product tests.

A repeatable way to choose

  1. Start with a primary source

    Collect the course notes, official reading list or a trusted source before asking an assistant to explain it.

  2. Ask for practice questions

    Prompt: “Create five quiz questions from these notes, include answers separately, and tell me which passages support each answer.”

  3. Check citations and arithmetic

    Open the quoted sources and recompute formulas yourself. AI links and page references may be incorrect.

  4. Create a revision log

    Record topics you misunderstood and write a short explanation in your own words after checking the material.

Things to double-check

  • Follow institution policies about AI assistance and academic integrity.
  • Do not upload student records or unpublished class materials without permission.
  • Summaries can omit nuance and assistants can invent citations.
