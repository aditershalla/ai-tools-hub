ABOUT THE PROJECT

Helping you find and use AI tools with clarity.

AI Tools Hub is an independent directory of AI products alongside seven practical writing and text utilities. Our aim is to help you decide which service to explore, and finish small tasks without unnecessary complexity.

Directory, not a product endorsement

Our Top 100 is an editorial discovery list, not an independently audited ranking by user count, traffic or performance. We describe possible use cases and limitations. Provider names, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Product capabilities, prices and plans change, so verify details on each official website.

We do not claim to have personally tested every product. Listings are informational, not paid recommendations unless clearly labelled in the future.

Which tools stay on your device?

Browser-only utilities: Word Counter, Case Converter, JSON Formatter, Password Maker, URL Encoder and Text Cleaner operate locally in the browser. Input in these utilities is not sent to our AI API by the website code.

AI Writer is different: when you press Generate, your prompt is transmitted to a Cloudflare Pages backend and sent to a model through Cloudflare Workers AI to produce a response. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information. Read the privacy notice before using it.

How is the directory maintained?

Items are grouped by broad tasks such as writing, image creation, coding and research. Our short descriptions provide starting points, not guarantees of suitability. Feedback about broken links, inaccurate descriptions or accessibility problems is welcome through the contact page.
